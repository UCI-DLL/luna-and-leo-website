Research

Built on Research. Designed for Young Learners.

For more than six years, our team has studied how dialogic AI — technology designed to engage children in meaningful back-and-forth conversation — can support early learning.

Instead of simply presenting information, conversational agents can act as interactive reading partners: asking questions, responding to children's answers, providing feedback, and encouraging children to explain their thinking.

Our Approach Our Findings Publications

Our Approach

Why Conversation Matters

Children learn through conversation.

Dialogic reading is an interactive approach to shared reading that engages children in back-and-forth conversation about a story. A reading partner asks questions, listens to children's responses, and provides feedback based on what they say.

AI as an Interactive Reading Partner

More than listening or reading.

Within The Science Adventures of Luna & Leo, these conversational moments are designed to reinforce science concepts and vocabulary while encouraging children to actively engage with what they are learning.

Our Findings

Past Research

Promising results for dialogic AI.

Our previous research has found promising results for the use of dialogic AI with young children.

Our research has also demonstrated that conversational agents can effectively engage children during reading and can provide benefits comparable to those of a human reader using the same dialogic approach.

What's Next

Bringing this work into classrooms.

We are now bringing this work into TK–K classrooms to better understand how bilingual, interactive science e-books can support science learning and complement teachers' existing instructional practices.
