About the Platform

Science Adventures of Luna & Leo

Bilingual science stories designed to get young learners reading, talking, and thinking.

A collection of interactive English-Spanish science e-books designed for young learners.

Through engaging stories, children explore foundational science concepts while practicing skills such as observing, asking questions, making predictions, and explaining their ideas.

The e-books were created in close alignment with early learning science standards, introducing foundational science concepts and practices through engaging, age-appropriate stories. By bringing science and literacy together, the platform creates meaningful opportunities for children to learn through reading, conversation, and exploration.

  • Grades: TK–2
  • Languages: English & Spanish
  • Format: Interactive e-books with a conversational companion

Platform Features

Bilingual by Design

Stories are available in English and Spanish, supporting science learning across languages.

Science Through Storytelling

Children encounter science concepts through engaging narratives that encourage them to observe, predict, question, and reason about the world around them.

Interactive Conversations

At key moments throughout each story, a conversational agent asks children questions about science concepts and vocabulary and responds to their ideas.

These conversations encourage children to explain their thinking while reinforcing important concepts from the story.
