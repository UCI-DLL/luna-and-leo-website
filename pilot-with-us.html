We are inviting Transitional Kindergarten teachers to participate in our bilingual science e-book pilot.

Participating teachers will receive access to 24 bilingual English–Spanish science e-books designed to help young learners explore science through stories, conversation, and inquiry.

As a Pilot Teacher, You'll:

  • Integrate The Science Adventures of Luna & Leo e-books into your classroom in ways that best meet your instructional needs.
  • Help our team communicate with families about their optional use of the e-books.
  • Welcome occasional classroom observations while the e-books are being used.
  • Participate in a one-hour monthly after-school meeting to share feedback on the e-books and suggest improvements.

What You'll Receive

24 Bilingual Science E-Books

Receive free access to 24 English–Spanish science e-books designed for Transitional Kindergarten and aligned with early learning science standards.

Professional Development

Receive three hours of professional development to support successful implementation of the e-books in your classroom.

A Voice in the Design

Your classroom experience and feedback will help our team understand what works for teachers and students, and shape future versions of the platform.

Join us in piloting The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo — and help your students see themselves as scientists, in their own language. If you're interested, email us at LunaAndLeoScience@gmail.com to get in touch.
