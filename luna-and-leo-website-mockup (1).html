The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo

Bilingual AI storybooks for young scientists
Luna and Leo, exploring the world together Illustrated storybook scene

Every adventure follows Luna and Leo, two curious kids who discover that science is all around them; at the lake near home, on a long car ride, or out in the garden.

Focused on California's PTKLF and Next Generation Science Standards
Where Learning Happens

Designed for school.
Made for discovery at home.

The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo extends early science learning beyond a single setting, helping young children explore, wonder, and build scientific understanding wherever they learn. Through culturally relevant Spanish-English stories, children connect science to their families, communities, and the world around them.

01 · For School

Science learning that fits the classroom.

Designed for TK–2 science learning, The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo aligns with California's PTKLF Science standards and K–2 Next Generation Science Standards. The e-books can be used during science instruction, classroom rotations, or independent learning centers, engaging children in age-appropriate scientific ideas through storytelling, exploration, and conversation.

02 · For Home

Science stories to read, explore, and discuss together.

Accessible from any internet-connected phone, tablet, or computer, The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo brings science learning home through culturally relevant stories children can enjoy with parents and caregivers. Family discussion questions at the end of each story encourage children to share what they noticed, ask questions, and connect scientific ideas to their everyday lives.

A cross-campus collaboration

Research, community, and learning

The Science Adventures of Luna and Leo brings together researchers, educators, families, and community partners to create meaningful early science learning experiences.

01 · Research

Built through cross-campus collaboration.

Created with researchers from the University of California, Irvine Digital Learning Lab, the University of Michigan, En Nuestra Lengua, and Harvard University.

02 · Community

Designed with communities at the center.

Together with community partners, these teams are creating culturally relevant, language-rich science learning experiences for young children.
